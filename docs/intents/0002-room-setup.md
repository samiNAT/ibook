# Intent 0002 — Room setup

Derived from PRD requirement 0002, rank 2.

PROBLEM:  A manager who has just signed up has an empty product and no way to represent
          their real meeting rooms without contacting anyone.
USER:     A newly signed-up facilities manager, still inside the first onboarding
          session.
OUTCOME:  Every physical room the manager cares about (5–20 of them) exists in the app
          with a name and a seat capacity, and immediately shows up on the live
          availability screen.
SUCCESS:  A manager who begins onboarding adds at least one room and reaches the
          availability screen within the same session, measured from onboarding
          analytics.
LIMITS:   No hardware, no per-room device pairing, no IT ticket — must be doable
          entirely by the manager from a browser.
NOT NOW:  Zones/floors/buildings grouping, room amenities/equipment metadata, per-room
          approval rules.
